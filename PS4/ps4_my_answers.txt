## q2_discussion
# Q2 - Discuss differences in prices across age groups.
As children get older, prices fall for childcare, as seen by the means. Also, since the CI's don't overlap, this is statistically significant difference, meaning not by chance. This could be due to lower costs for childcare of older children as compared with younger ones.

## q3_discussion
# Q3 - What has happened to childcare prices over this period?
Nominal prices for childcare over this period rose, however real prices remained fairly flat. Rising a little through 2020 but then decreasing by 2022.

## q4d_interpretation
# Q4(d) - Do you reject H0, and why? What happens with 2015 data and subsidy levels?
I reject H₀ because the test stat (6.39) in 2022 elicits an extremely small p-value. Because the t stat is about one fourth of the t stat from 2015, meaning that the voucher was much lower than the average price. I also reject the 2015 null hypothesis due to the t-stat.
